Shipping

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States. Shipping is free on every order.

Order confirmation

Right after you order you get an email with your order number and a summary. If it does not arrive within an hour, check your spam folder or write to hello@plushsweep.com.

Processing time

We pack and hand over your order within 1 to 3 business days.

Tracking

As soon as your order ships, you get a second email with a tracking link.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive 7 to 12 days after shipping. Packing takes 1 to 3 business days before that. Order today, arrives Nov 16 to Nov 23. Order by Dec 8 for Christmas delivery.

Carriers

Every order ships with tracking. You see the carrier in your shipping email.

Delays

If a parcel is late, we tell you before you have to ask and help you track it down. If it is lost, we send a new one or refund you.

Contact

Questions about your delivery: hello@plushsweep.com, answered within 24 hours on business days.

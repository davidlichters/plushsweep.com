Returns and refunds

Our guarantee

60-day money-back guarantee. If the glove doesn't work for you, email us within 60 days of delivery and we refund the full price. No need to send it back.

Who can ask for a refund

Anyone who bought from us. The glove does not have to be unused, and sale prices are covered too.

How to start

Email hello@plushsweep.com with your order number and one line on what did not work. That is all.

Damaged or wrong item

Send us a photo within 14 days of delivery and we send a replacement or refund you, your choice. You never pay shipping for our mistake.

Cancellations

Cancel any time before your order ships for a full refund.

When the money arrives

We issue the refund within 3 business days of your email. Your bank or card usually shows it 5 to 10 business days later.

Exchanges

There is one size, so there is nothing to exchange. If you want a different set, we refund the first order and you place a new one.

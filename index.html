Questions

How do I use it?

Slip it on, then rub the fabric back and forth in short strokes along one line. The hair gathers into a strip. Peel the strip off by hand.

Which surfaces does it work on?

Best on upholstery and thicker fabric: sofas, beds and sheets, area rugs, carpets and car seats. On thin clothes a quick pass works, but a lint roller is finer there.

How do I clean it?

Rinse it under running water and let it air dry. It needs no refills and no batteries.
